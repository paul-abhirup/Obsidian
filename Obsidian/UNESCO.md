**Project Name (working idea):** _StorySphere – Collaborative Digital Storytelling for Responsible Media_

**Goal:**  
To create an accessible platform where youth, educators, and community storytellers can **collaboratively create and share digital stories**, while learning **responsible media practices (MIL principles)** in a hands-on way.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

This is where your project can really shine, because MIL Hackathons often ask “Who benefits?” Let’s pick **2–3 focused audiences** (instead of “everyone”):

1. **Students & Young Creators (ages 13–25):**
    
    - They’re the most active content creators (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).
    - But they often **lack awareness** of misinformation, copyright, and AI-generated risks.
    - The platform becomes both an **educational tool** and a **creative outlet**.
        
2. **Educators & Trainers:**
    
    - Teachers can use the platform for classroom projects.
    - They get built-in MIL modules (fact-checking, attribution challenges, responsible storytelling).
        
3. _(Optional secondary audience)_ **Community Journalists / NGOs:**
    
    - Grassroots reporters and NGOs can collaboratively create local impact stories with verified information.
    - Helps them counter misinformation while amplifying voices.
        

👉 By targeting **youth + educators**, you align strongly with UNESCO’s **focus on public-interest information and resilience through education**.

---

## ⚡ Core Features (Refined to MIL Theme)

1. **AI-Aware Storytelling Toolkit**
    
    - Web app where users combine text, images, audio, video.
    - AI assistant _suggests sources_, but with transparency labels: “This is AI-generated, fact-check before use.”
    - Encourages **critical evaluation of AI outputs**.
        
2. **Verification & Fact-Checking Nudges**
    
    - Built-in prompts: “Have you added a source for this claim?
    - One-click integration with fact-checking APIs (e.g., Google Fact Check Tools, PolitiFact, Wikipedia references).
    - Educates users to **pause and verify** before publishing.
        
3. **Copyright & Attribution Helper**
    
    - When users upload media, platform auto-suggests Creative Commons / free-to-use alternatives.
    - Teaches youth about **ethical media reuse**.
        
4. **Collaborative Story Rooms**
    
    - Multiple users edit the same story (like Google Docs).
    - Peer feedback system (highlight, comment, suggest)
    - Builds teamwork + teaches respectful digital interaction.
        
5. **Digital Citizenship Challenges**
    
    - Mini-modules built into the platform:
        
        - _“Spot the Deepfake” challenge_
        - _“Find the Bias in This Article” quiz_
        - _“Source Attribution Hunt” mini-game_
            
    - Gamifies MIL learning.

## 🌍 Impact & Alignment with UNESCO Theme

- **New Digital Frontiers (Generative AI):**  
    Instead of banning AI, your platform **teaches users how to responsibly use and question AI-generated content**.
    
- **Public-Interest Information:**  
    The stories created can focus on local/global social issues—climate change, health, human rights—making them more than just “fun content.”
    
- **Critical Thinking:**  
    Every feature (fact-checking, copyright guidance, feedback) subtly trains users to question, evaluate, and act responsibly.
    
- **Inclusive & Resilient Ecosystem:**
    
    - Open-source, web-based, mobile-friendly.
    - Multilingual UI (using AI translation APIs).
    - Accessibility features (screen reader compatibility, captions).

## 🛠 Tech Stack (Practical for Hackathon MVP)

- **Frontend:** React (fast, hackathon-friendly).
- **Backend:** Node.js (Express) for story management.
- **Database:** MongoDB / Firebase (easy setup for collaborative editing).
- **Media Storage:** Cloudinary / Firebase Storage.
- **AI Integration:** OpenAI API / HuggingFace models for:->
    - Fact-check suggestions,
    - AI content warnings,
    - Translation.
        
- **Collaboration:** WebSockets (real-time editing like Google Docs).
